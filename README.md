# Electricity Access in Uganda, 2000–2023

## Project question
How did the percentage of Uganda’s population with access to electricity change between 2000 and 2023?

## Data source
World Bank Data360: “Percentage of population with access to electricity,” from the World Bank Group Corporate Scorecard.
[View the source data on World Bank Data360](https://data360.worldbank.org/en/indicator/WB_CSC_EG_ELC_ACCS_ZS)

- Indicator code: WB_CSC_EG_ELC_ACCS_ZS
- Country: Uganda
- Period analyzed: 2000–2023
- Observations: 24 annual values
- Sources listed by the dataset: World Bank, International Energy Agency (IEA), and UN-Energy

## Tools and method
I used Microsoft Excel to organize Uganda’s annual data into two columns, create a line chart, and calculate the change between the first and last years.

The Excel formula was =B25-B2.

## Main findings
- Electricity access increased from 7.4% in 2000 to 51.5% in 2023.
- This represents an increase of 44.1 percentage points.
- The overall trend was upward, although access declined in some years.
- In 2023, 48.5% of the population remained without electricity access, calculated as 100 − 51.5.

## Limitations
National averages can hide differences between communities and between rural and urban areas. This analysis does not explain what caused the changes or measure electricity affordability or reliability. Findings reflect the downloaded dataset covering 2000–2023.

## What I learned
I practiced organizing data in Excel, choosing the correct chart axes, and distinguishing percentage points from percentage growth.
